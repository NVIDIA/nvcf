## Self-Managed 0.6.2 (legacy patch)

Self-Managed 0.6.2 is a Helmfile bundle patch for the legacy 0.6.x stack.
It updates the OpenBao chart from 0.30.25 to 0.32.6 and refreshes the JWT plugin
catalog before migrations. Runtime image versions remain at their 0.6.1 pins.

Follow the [0.6.1 to 0.6.2 upgrade notes](../../docs/overview/release-notes/0.6.1-to-0.6.2-upgrade.md)
before the staged migration to 1.0.1. See the
[stable release](https://github.com/NVIDIA/nvcf/releases/tag/deploy/stacks/self-managed/v0.6.2)
and its [artifact inventory](https://github.com/NVIDIA/nvcf/releases/download/deploy/stacks/self-managed/v0.6.2/nvcf-self-managed-stack-inventory.json)
for the patch contents.
